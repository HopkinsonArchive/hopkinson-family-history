The Hopkinson Family History documents more than four centuries through genealogical research, biographies, vital records, censuses, probate files, military records, newspapers, cemetery records, town histories, and surviving family evidence.

The currently reconstructed lineage begins with **[[Michael Hopkinson]]**, an early settler of Rowley, Massachusetts, and continues through documented descendants into the twentieth century.

> [!important] Research in progress
> This is a documentary research archive. It does not assume that every published relationship, date, or biographical detail has been proven conclusively.
>
> Claims are classified as **confirmed**, **strong inference**, **unverified**, or **unresolved**. Conclusions will be revised when stronger evidence is found.

## Working Family Line

The archive presently follows this corrigible research line:

1. **[[Michael Hopkinson]]** (c. 1600–1648/49)
2. **[[Caleb Hopkinson Sr.]]** (1648/49–1721)
3. **[[Caleb Hopkinson Jr.]]** (1681–1730)
4. **[[Samuel Hopkinson]]** (1718–1785)
5. **[[Silas Hopkinson]]** (1763–1828)
6. **[[Uriah Hopkinson]]** (1791–1876)
7. **[[Ann Matilda Hopkinson]]** (born 1817; death not yet confirmed)
8. **[[Lowell Herbert Hopkinson]]** (1836–1904)
9. **[[Harry Francis Hopkinson]]** (1868–1897)
10. **[[Ralph L. Hopkinson Sr.]]** (1894–1946)
11. **[[Ralph L. Hopkinson Jr.]]** (1920–2000)

Dates, places, relationships, and name forms shown in this summary remain subject to correction as individual records are examined.

## Lineage and Surname Distinction

The documented colonial Hopkinson descent reaches Lowell Herbert Hopkinson through his mother:

**[[Uriah Hopkinson]] → [[Ann Matilda Hopkinson]] → [[Lowell Herbert Hopkinson]]**

Lowell’s surviving Bradford birth record identifies Ann Matilda as his mother but does not name his biological father. Lowell inherited and retained the Hopkinson surname from her.

Ann Matilda and **[[William Pillsbury Bailey]]** filed a marriage intention on May 7, 1836, while Ann was pregnant and 39 days before Lowell’s recorded birth on June 15, 1836. No completed marriage or direct paternity record has yet been found.

The archive treats William as Lowell’s biological father by **strong circumstantial inference**:

**[[William Pillsbury Bailey]] → [[Lowell Herbert Hopkinson]]**

The record limitation and the working conclusion are both preserved. The complete lineage should not be described as an uninterrupted male-line or Y-chromosome Hopkinson descent.

## Evidence Classification

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Directly supported by reliable records |
| **Strong inference** | Compelling evidence supports the conclusion, but direct proof has not been found |
| **Unverified** | Reported in family material or secondary sources but not independently confirmed |
| **Unresolved** | Evidence is missing, incomplete, insufficient, or conflicting |

Primary records receive priority, including:

- Vital records
- Census records
- Probate and court files
- Military and pension records
- Church and town records
- Cemetery records
- Contemporary newspapers

Published genealogies, unsourced online trees, and family traditions are treated as research leads unless independently supported.

## Current Research Areas

Research presently includes:

- Michael Hopkinson’s English origins and parentage
- The documentary links between the Caleb, Samuel, Silas, and Uriah generations
- Family traditions concerning Revolutionary War or Bunker Hill service
- Uriah Hopkinson’s occupation
- Whether Uriah helped teach Lowell the shoemaking trade
- Ann Matilda Hopkinson’s later life and death
- Additional evidence concerning William Pillsbury Bailey and Lowell’s paternity
- Lowell’s military and pension records
- The onset and cause of Lowell’s later paralysis and complete disability
- Biographical and parent-child documentation for the later deceased generations
