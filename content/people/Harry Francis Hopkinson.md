Harry Francis Hopkinson was born in Groveland, Massachusetts, on 4 October 1868, the son of Lowell Herbert Hopkinson and Margaret Metcalf. He lived only twenty-nine years, dying in Groveland on 1 November 1897. The surviving town and cemetery records establish the principal outline of his life, although his cause of death remains unknown pending examination of the full death record.

## Family

Harry married Flora Barnhill, apparently at Haverhill, Massachusetts, about 1890. The date and place are strongly suggested by a marginal annotation in Harry's Groveland birth record, but the original marriage return has not yet been located.

Harry and Flora had at least two children:

- **Marion Maude Hopkinson**, born 16 June 1892 in Groveland; died 5 February 1898, aged five, and buried in Preeper Hill Cemetery, Shubenacadie, Nova Scotia.
- **Ralph L. Hopkinson**, born 11 February 1894 in Groveland. The original town register leaves the child's given name blank, but later records identify him as Ralph. In 1901 he was living in Shubenacadie with his widowed mother and his Barnhill grandparents.

## Death and burial

Harry died in Groveland on 1 November 1897. The town record describes him as married and gives his age as 29 years and 28 days, which agrees exactly with his recorded birth date. Groveland's annual report lists the same death but gives 29 years and 26 days, apparently a minor copying or calculation error. The report names Groveland as both the place of death and place of burial, but does not state a cause of death.

Harry is buried in **Riverview Cemetery, Groveland, Massachusetts**, in **Holly 1, plot 290**. The same family plot contains records for his parents, Lowell H. and Margaret Hopkinson, and several other Hopkinson relatives. The cemetery portal is useful evidence for the burial location and family grouping, although some dates in its database are incomplete or inaccurate.

Harry's death was followed only 96 days later by the death of his five-year-old daughter Marion in Nova Scotia. By the 1901 Canadian census, Flora was recorded as a widow in the Shubenacadie household of her parents, Isaac and Hannah Barnhill, with her son Ralph. These records show that Flora returned to her family in Nova Scotia after Harry's death; they do not establish why Harry died.

## What remains unresolved

The most important unanswered question is **Harry's cause of death at age twenty-nine**. Neither the Groveland annual report nor the presently available town transcription supplies it. A complete copy of his 1 November 1897 death record should be requested from the Groveland Town Clerk, specifically asking for every field on the original record, including cause of death, occupation, attending physician, and burial details.

The other worthwhile missing record is the original Haverhill marriage return, probably from 1890. It may supply Harry's occupation and residence and confirm Flora's age, birthplace, and parents.

No defensible online obituary or probate entry has yet been found.
